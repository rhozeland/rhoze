Our story · Since 2016

Rhozeland

A creator-owned content house. Built through friendship, shaped by culture, and grounded in the work we make together.

We fuel creators, grow their brands, and turn ideas into impact.

Artists, brands, founders, and builders have shaped moments together with Rhozeland. We bring audio, visuals, and design together—from recording sessions and music videos to campaign content and releases.

Our mission is to make it easier for independent creators to find their people, make ambitious work, and build something that lasts. The studio is the foundation. The platform extends those connections beyond its walls.

The Rhozeland token.

$RHOZE · Solana

A connection to the house.

$RHOZE is Rhozeland's token on Solana. It connects our creative ecosystem with on-chain participation, including verified holder access on projects that offer it.

You don’t need to buy a token to discover work, create a project, or connect with creators. Pairing a token with a project is optional.

What exists today

Optional project token pairing, wallet-verified holder access, and a contribution ledger with Creator Pass progress. Recorded rewards are not the same as tokens already delivered to your wallet.

What we’re working toward

A creator-led economy where creative work can help fund more creative work. Buybacks, burns, grants, and broader revenue-sharing ideas are ambitions—not guaranteed benefits or automatic payouts.

A token is not company equity, a claim on studio revenue, or a promise of returns. Crypto prices can fluctuate significantly and you could lose the full amount you spend. Holder benefits depend on the individual project.
